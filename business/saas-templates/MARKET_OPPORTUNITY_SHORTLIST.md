# Market Opportunity Shortlist Template

## Purpose

Compare a small set of product opportunities using customer evidence,
workflow frequency, delivery constraints, and a measurable validation step.
Keep actual venture ideas, personal interests, health context, customer
records, and founder-fit assessments in the configured private repository.

## Comparison Criteria

For each candidate, record privately:

1. Customer and repeated problem: who experiences it, and when?
2. Existing alternatives: how is the problem handled today?
3. Evidence: which interviews, observed behavior, or public sources support it?
4. Data requirements: what is needed, and can it be obtained with permission?
5. Delivery constraints: what security, privacy, maintenance, or regulatory
   requirements affect a first usable version?
6. Validation: what small experiment could disprove the central assumption?

## Shortlist Format

| Candidate | Customer problem | Evidence | Main uncertainty | Next experiment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Candidate A | Specific recurring problem | Source or observation | Untested assumption | Small measurable test |
| Candidate B | Specific recurring problem | Source or observation | Untested assumption | Small measurable test |

Avoid presenting inferred personal interests as evidence of product demand.
Separate public market facts from private founder-fit judgments.

## Publication Boundary

Public examples use synthetic inputs. Publish a real opportunity analysis
only after its owner approves the exact material and private customer,
account, personal, and operating context has been removed. Keep original
requests and private decision records in the private repository.
